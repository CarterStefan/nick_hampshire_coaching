# Direction | nickhampshire.com

Confirmed 25 November 2026. Read this before any unslop run on the site and keep to it.

- **Product:** Nick coaches leaders and teams to speak with confidence and energy, through workshops, 1:1 coaching and event hosting.
- **Character:** warm, assured, energetic.
- **World:** the stage and the room: house lights down, one light on the speaker, the front row leaning in.
- **Moments:** real photography of Nick working a room. The hero photo is the bright element; everything around it stays quiet.
- **Signature:** teal as the spotlight. It falls on one phrase or one action per screen and nowhere else.

**Kind of surface:** Persuade (personal-brand marketing site).

**Ramp:** dark ink tinted purple. Page `#0F0A1A`, bands and footer `#201533`, cards `#2C1E45`, inputs `#1C1430`. Text is off-white `#F9F9FE` at 95% (body) and 72% (muted). Borders are off-white at 8 to 30%.

**Accent:** teal `#9FEDED`, about 10% of any screen. It's used for the main call to action, links, focus, the nav underline and one highlighted phrase. Borders, labels, eyebrows and headings stay neutral. The bright purple `#7A3EE3` is retired.

**Type:** Elms Sans throughout. Headings weight 600, body 400, sentence case everywhere, no uppercase tracked labels.

**Rules specific to this site:**
- One solid teal button per section at most, and the label is always "Book a free call".
- Buttons carry no arrows.
- The testimonial carousel moves only when a visitor uses its controls.

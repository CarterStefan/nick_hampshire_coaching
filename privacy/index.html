Privacy

This page explains what happens to the details you share on nickhampshire.com.

Last updated November 2026.

Who is responsible

Nick Hampshire is responsible for the personal information collected through this website.

What is collected and why

When you use the contact form, you share your name, email address, the area you'd like help with and your message. These details are used only to reply to your enquiry and to discuss working together. They are not sold, and they are not used for marketing unless you ask for the newsletter.

The form is delivered by Web3Forms, which passes your message on by email.

Newsletter

The contact form has an optional box to receive Nick's newsletter: practical ideas on confidence, energy and speaking with impact, roughly once a month. The box starts unticked, and you can send an enquiry without ticking it.

If you tick it, your name and email address are used to send you the newsletter. This is based on your consent. A record of your choice and the wording you agreed to is kept with your enquiry, so there is proof of when you signed up.

The newsletter is sent through Substack, so your email address is added to Nick's Substack mailing list and is also handled under Substack's own privacy policy. Every issue has an unsubscribe link. You can also withdraw consent at any time through the contact form. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything sent before you did.

Booking a call

The "Book a free call" links open Calendly. Anything you enter there is handled under Calendly's own privacy policy.

Third-party content

  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and styles and icons from the jsDelivr network. These services receive your IP address when your browser requests them.
  • The video on the About page only loads from YouTube (in privacy-enhanced mode) when you press play.

This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

How long details are kept

Enquiry details are kept for as long as needed to respond and, if we work together, for the length of that working relationship. If you sign up for the newsletter, your email address is kept on the mailing list until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details you've shared, or object to how they are used, by sending a message through the contact form. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK or the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos in Spain.

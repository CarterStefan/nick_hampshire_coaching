Meet Nick on stage

I'm a communication coach, keynote speaker and event host, and I bring energy, warmth and insight to every stage. With a background in leadership, coaching and performance, I help audiences connect ideas to action, whether I'm leading a keynote, moderating a panel or hosting a live event.

My style combines authenticity and humour with a deep understanding of human communication, so audiences leave inspired and equipped to make real change.

As an event host or MC, I make sure every moment moves smoothly: setting the tone, handling transitions between segments, moderating panels, introducing speakers, keeping to the timeline and engaging the audience. Whether it's a senior leadership conference, a high-profile awards night or a charity gala, I bring clarity, calm confidence and warm humour to make the event memorable.

Professional event hosting

From conferences to charity galas, I bring structure, humour and a natural connection with audiences, so events flow smoothly and memorably. I host for multinational corporations, non-profit and charity galas, fundraising events, awards evenings, panel discussions and live moderated interviews.

  • Full event facilitation: openers, closers, transitions, introducing speakers and managing surprises
  • Panel moderation and live interviews, with a natural flow and Q&A that includes the audience
  • Liaising with organisers behind the scenes to keep to time, adjust on the fly and troubleshoot
  • Tailored tone (formal, playful or inspiring) aligned to your brand values and audience
  • Energy control: reading the room, keeping momentum high and making sure downtime is purposeful too
